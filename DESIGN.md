# The Crystal Dragonfly Design System

## Theme

A calm consultation room translated into a focused education-services website. Forest green provides authority, antique gold marks important details, pale blue-gray creates breathing room, and natural photography keeps the practice personal.

## Color

- Forest: `oklch(0.30 0.055 158)`
- Forest deep: `oklch(0.22 0.042 158)`
- Gold: `oklch(0.67 0.12 78)`
- Blue-gray: `oklch(0.93 0.018 220)`
- Canvas: `oklch(0.985 0.004 95)`
- Ink: `oklch(0.23 0.02 155)`
- Muted ink: `oklch(0.43 0.022 155)`

## Typography

Use Georgia for display copy to match the approved reference's established, bookish voice. Use Avenir Next with a system sans-serif fallback for body copy and controls.

## Components

Buttons are compact, slightly rounded, and action-specific. Service cards use restrained borders and varied accent colors. FAQ uses native disclosure controls. The contact form uses a branded shell with clear fields and status messaging.

## Layout

Multi-page, mobile-first composition with a sticky header, image-led page headers, centered introduction, image-led expectations band, biography, service grid, FAQ, and contact form.

## Motion

One restrained page entrance, menu transitions, and direct interaction feedback. Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential movement.
