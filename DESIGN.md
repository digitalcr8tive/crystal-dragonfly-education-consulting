# The Crystal Dragonfly Design System

## Theme

The official Crystal Dragonfly identity translated into a clear education-services website. Deep navy provides authority, steel blue carries the logo's sense of movement, cool mist creates breathing room, and natural photography keeps the practice personal.

## Color

- Logo navy: `#123550`
- Marine navy: `#234E6D`
- Logo steel blue: `#3E6D8C`
- Cool mist: `#EFF5F8`
- Ink: `oklch(0.28 0.042 242)`
- Muted ink: `oklch(0.47 0.035 240)`

## Typography

Use the locally hosted Montserrat family throughout. Light display weights echo the official wordmark, while medium and semibold weights keep body copy, navigation, and controls clear.

## Components

Buttons are compact, slightly rounded, and action-specific. Service cards use restrained borders and varied accent colors. FAQ uses native disclosure controls. The contact form uses a branded shell with clear fields and status messaging.

## Layout

Multi-page, mobile-first composition with a sticky header, image-led page headers, centered introduction, image-led expectations band, biography, service grid, FAQ, and contact form.

## Motion

One restrained page entrance, menu transitions, and direct interaction feedback. Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential movement.
