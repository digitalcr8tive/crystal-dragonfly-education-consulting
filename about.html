About Crystal

Experience grounded in people, learning, and resilient systems.

Crystal Green-Braswell brings more than two decades of educational leadership to every conversation.

Professional Biography

A career built across classrooms, schools, and systems.

Crystal Green-Braswell is an educational leader in Arkansas with over 24 years of experience. She currently serves as a district leader for the Little Rock School District and is the owner of The Crystal Dragonfly Education Consulting.

Throughout her career, Crystal has served as a state leadership development coach, academic achievement specialist, assistant principal, and classroom teacher at both elementary and secondary levels.

Her work centers student growth, social and emotional learning, and learning systems that are healing- and resilience-centered. She helps educators, families, and organizations connect thoughtful planning with practical action.

As a contributing author of The School Wellness Wheel, Crystal brings a systems-minded approach to creating conditions where students and educators can learn, lead, and grow.

Service Approach

Thoughtful guidance begins with understanding.

The process begins with careful listening: the learner’s history, the family’s concerns, the school context, and the outcome that would make support feel useful.

Clarity comes before a plan. Complex concerns are organized into focused priorities, practical options, and next steps that can be understood and used.

Recommendations are built for real settings. The goal is stronger confidence, communication, and learning systems that continue to help after the first conversation.
