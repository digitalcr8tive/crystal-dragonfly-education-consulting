About Crystal

Experience grounded in people, learning, and resilient systems.

Crystal Green-Braswell brings more than 27 years of educational leadership, culture work, and holistic wellness to every conversation.

Professional Biography

A career built across classrooms, schools, and systems.

Crystal Green-Braswell is an experienced educational leader who serves as Coordinator of Staff Wellness and Culture for the Little Rock School District. She is also the owner of The Crystal Dragonfly Education Consulting.

Across more than 27 years in education, Crystal has served as Director of Culture, Climate & Well-Being, Coordinator of Staff Wellness & Culture, State Leadership Development Coach in Arkansas, Academic Achievement Specialist, Assistant Principal, and classroom teacher at both elementary and secondary levels.

Her work focuses on student growth, adult self-awareness, social and emotional learning, and the culture surrounding teaching and learning. She uses healing and resilience as a foundation for helping educators, leaders, and individuals create change that can hold over time.

As a contributing author of The School Wellness Wheel, Crystal brings systems knowledge together with holistic wellness. Her goal is to leave a lasting, positive legacy through compassionate leadership and practical support for the people who shape learning communities.

Service Approach

Thoughtful guidance begins with understanding.

The work begins with awareness: seeing the patterns, pressures, and possibilities that shape a person, team, or learning environment.

Well-being is treated as infrastructure. In organizations, that means looking beyond occasional wellness activities to schedules, meeting design, workload, feedback, and leadership practice.

Change is designed for real life. Whether the work is organizational or personal, the focus stays on small, repeatable shifts that remain useful after the first conversation.
