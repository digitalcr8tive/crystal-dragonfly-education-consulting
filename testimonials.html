Testimonials

Growth seen through the people living it.

Two personal reflections on mentorship, self-awareness, healing, and the changes that continue beyond one conversation.

Client Reflection

Teresa Lucille

Shortly after my ex-husband left me and our two children for the fifth time and finally filed for divorce, I began to spiral. As a preacher’s kid, a licensed minister, a school administrator, and an individual with four degrees, including one in counseling, and only three classes away from completing the requirements toward becoming a Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC), I felt like a failure.

I had always been the one expected to be strong. I was the person others depended on, the one who encouraged everyone else and somehow found the strength to keep going. But when I needed someone to be strong for me, it felt as though no one was there except my mother, until I met Crystal Green-Braswell, whom I affectionately call “Lady C.”

I truly believe Lady C was a God-sent angel because our paths crossed at the very moment I needed to be resuscitated emotionally and spiritually. What began as a state- and district-selected mentorship soon blossomed into a genuine friendship, rooted in our shared desire to empower today’s students to become the best versions of themselves through education and positive social-emotional awareness.

As we worked together, something unexpected began to happen. I slowly started removing the cape I had worn for years, the cape that represented strength but also concealed layers of unaddressed hurt. Our conversations often centered on strategies and techniques educators could use to support students who had experienced trauma. Yet, while I was gathering information and tools to help my teachers better serve their students, I found myself receiving healing, insight, and strength for my own life.

In many ways, I came seeking resources for others and discovered that God was providing restoration for me.

Crystal Green-Braswell is truly a treasure, not only to the educational community but to every person whose life she touches. She possesses a genuine heart and an authentic desire to see people become whole in mind, body, and spirit and empowered to operate at their fullest potential.

Lady C entered my life as a mentor, became my sister-friend, and, without even realizing it, helped me find the strength to remove my cape and begin healing the person underneath it. For that, I will always be grateful.

Client Reflection

Amber Wright

Since I’ve known Crystal Green-Braswell, my life’s journey has been significantly impacted. She unquestionably embodies what pursuing and maintaining wellness looks like through self-awareness, trauma-based recognition, and transformative practical lifestyle routines.

She is a seasoned, determined human being who is still learning, gaining many nuggets of wisdom, and sharing with others. She is an accountability partner who has not only endured a lot through her personal journey but has set out to share some of her losses and many of her gains with others in some of the most humbling, thought-provoking, naturally infused, remedial, creative, and beautiful ways.

I have learned so much about myself, and I’m still peeling layers back by experiencing what it looks like to finally make time for me and pour into me, due to Crystal being a pivotal part of this realization.

I’m no Scrooge, but Crystal has literally taken me by the hand and heart and walked me through different areas and moments in my life that have been dormant, conditioned away, accepted, and suppressed. These have been shocking and even somewhat uncomfortable at times to face. Yet, with grace, understanding, and superhuman compassion, she has modeled practices that help encourage me to work on myself without shame or judgment and without observing myself through a destructive lens.

She is one golden, evolving playbook, allowing so many willing others to read the pages and be inspired to make necessary shifts in their lives to live a purposeful, fulfilling, unregrettable life.
