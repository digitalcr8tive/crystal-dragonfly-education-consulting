Common questions

These answers provide a starting point. Specific recommendations and engagement details depend on the client’s needs.

What is education consulting?

Education consulting provides focused guidance around learning needs, school decisions, academic planning, family advocacy, or organizational improvement. The scope depends on the client and the outcome being pursued.

Who does Crystal work with?

Crystal works with K-12 students, high school students preparing for college, parents and caregivers, schools, districts, and education-focused organizations.

What happens after I reach out?

Your message helps identify the current concern, the desired outcome, relevant context, and useful next steps. Any recommended scope or follow-up support is explained clearly.

How should I prepare for the conversation?

Come prepared to describe the current concern, what has already been tried, and what you hope will be different. Relevant school or program information may be discussed when appropriate, but private records should only be shared through an approved secure process.

Is virtual support available?

Virtual support may be available depending on the service need and the format that best fits the client’s situation.

How is service pricing determined?

Pricing depends on the consultation type, scope, and level of ongoing support. Current details will be discussed before a client commits to continued work.

Can schools and organizations request customized support?

Yes. School and organization consulting begins with a discovery conversation, followed by a tailored scope for workshops, planning, systems support, or professional learning.

How do I contact Crystal?

Use the Contact page to share your name, contact information, service interest, and a short message about the support you are seeking.

Have a question specific to your situation?

Send a message with a few details, and Crystal can respond with the best next step.
