Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful details before we talk.

Learn how organizational work and personal coaching differ, what formats are available, and how to begin.

Common questions

These answers provide a starting point. Specific recommendations and engagement details depend on the client’s needs.

Review all services
Can I work with Crystal if I am not connected to a school?

Yes. Individual coaching is open to educators, professionals in any field, and people navigating transitions. The organizational and personal sides share a philosophy, not a prerequisite.

Does Crystal work with organizations other than schools?

Yes. Adult self-awareness, well-being as infrastructure, and people-centered culture work can support nonprofits, healthcare teams, government agencies, community organizations, and businesses as well as schools and districts.

What is the difference between the two sides of the work?

Organizational transformation focuses on the climate of a shared environment by developing the adults within it. Personal transformation focuses on an individual’s habits, well-being, alignment, and the long arc of the life they are building.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching is forward-focused thought partnership around goals, habits, and how you want to live. It is not clinical treatment, mental health care, or medical advice. When a need calls for another professional, Crystal will say so directly.

Do I need to know exactly what I want before reaching out?

No. A first conversation can help name what is happening and what a useful outcome would look like. Clarity is often one of the first results of the process.

How long does an engagement last?

It depends on the work. Some engagements are a single session. Others continue across a semester, school year, or coaching cycle. Length, sequence, and cadence are shaped together.

Is virtual support available?

Yes. Coaching, workshops, and most organizational work can be delivered virtually or in a hybrid format. Retreats and curated travel are offered in person.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on scope, format, and duration. Share what you hope to accomplish through the Contact page, and Crystal can prepare a proposal for that context.

How do we start?

Start with an initial conversation. Organizations typically begin with a scoping discussion about goals and existing systems. Individuals begin with a discovery conversation to clarify what they want and whether the partnership is a good fit.

Have a question specific to your situation?

Send a message with a few details, and Crystal can respond with the best next step.
