# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students, parents, families, school leaders, and education organizations seeking clear academic guidance, planning support, and systems-focused consultation.

## Product Purpose

Introduce The Crystal Dragonfly Education Consulting, establish Crystal Green-Braswell's credibility, explain available services, and turn qualified interest into contact-form inquiries.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, reassuring, and purposeful. The voice is clear, compassionate, and experienced without sounding institutional or overly promotional.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic school templates, childlike classroom decoration, corporate consulting jargon, fake testimonials, invented outcomes, and overly animated marketing layouts.

## Design Principles

1. Give families clarity before asking them to reach out.
2. Let Crystal's experience and approach carry the site's authority.
3. Keep service information practical and easy to compare.
4. Make contacting the business feel like a natural continuation of the website.
5. Be honest about content that is not ready for publication.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with readable type, strong contrast, visible focus states, semantic controls, reduced-motion support, and touch targets of at least 44 pixels.
